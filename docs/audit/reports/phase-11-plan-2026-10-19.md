## Why This Shape

The Phase 1 to 10 audit closed with all 18 ledger findings `fixed` or `verified`, no
accepted risks, and an explicit gap on every one of the 24 user workflows plus five
runtime conditions. Since then 113 files changed across nine commits: the PianoHero to
LumaKeys rename with a one-release data migration, a new static-file server with
byte-range support, MuseScore `.mscz` upload through a child process, a looping video
main menu, and pop-out interaction fixes. Nothing in the AppBridge contract changed.

A from-scratch audit would duplicate evidence that still holds. Phase 11 therefore has
three jobs, in order:

1. Audit the delta: every new surface gets a coverage row, an oracle, and evidence.
2. Re-verify the 18 fixed findings against the new identity using
   `templates/reverification-record.md`; a failed re-verification reopens the ID.
3. Close the runtime workflow gaps that Phase 10 left owed, as far as the available
   environments allow, and record the rest as `blocked` with an owner.

Discovery stays read-only outside `docs/audit/`. Leads below are not findings until
they pass `templates/finding.md` and a challenge.

## Human-In-The-Loop Policy

The owner asked that an agent do as much as possible and that manual tests be
streamlined rather than raised one at a time. Phase 11 therefore runs as follows:

- The agent owns every lane. The owner's only recurring role is manual verifier.
- Discovery is read-only, so it needs no approval gates. The agent does not ask
  before reading, building, running isolated servers, or writing under `docs/audit/`.
- When a row needs human proof, the agent marks it `blocked (user batch)`, appends a
  fully staged item to [phase-11-user-tests.md](phase-11-user-tests.md), and moves on.
  Items are grouped into sittings by what the owner must have in front of them, and
  a sitting is offered only when it is marked ready.
- The owner is asked for a decision only at three points: approving this plan (done),
  the end of discovery (which findings enter remediation), and anything that would
  rewrite git history or touch a production deployment.
- Remediation stays on a separate branch and is a separate authorization.
